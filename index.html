Privacy
Policy

drop. · Effective 31 October 2026

drop. ("the app") is a video and photo downloader made by an independent developer. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we don't collect any of your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no server of its own. We never receive your links, downloads, history or any personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • Download history and settings: stored in the app's private storage so the Library and Settings work.
  • Downloaded files: saved to your gallery in a "drop" folder. You can delete them like any other file.
  • Optional site sign-in: if you choose to sign in to a website inside the app (to save your own private or follower-only posts), that site's login cookies are kept only on your device and used only for requests to that same site. Your password goes directly to that website; the app never reads or stores it.

Network requests

When you download something, the app connects only to the website hosting the link you provided, over HTTPS. Those websites have their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state: to download the links you give it.
  • Notifications & foreground service: to show download progress and keep a download you started running.
  • Wake lock / Wi-Fi state: so downloads continue while the screen is off.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only): to save files to your gallery.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear downloads to erase history and Settings → Accounts → sign out to remove site sessions. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your phone.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Your responsibility

Only download content you own or have permission to use. drop. is not affiliated with any social network or video platform.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
